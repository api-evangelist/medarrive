# MedArrive

> MedArrive is a healthcare technology company providing a logistics and workforce platform for in-home care delivery. Health systems and in-home care providers use it for smart scheduling, real-time provider routing, automated re-routing and patient reminders, a live command-center dashboard, and a provider mobile app. The platform is HIPAA-compliant and is backed by Kleiner Perkins. No public developer API is currently published.
